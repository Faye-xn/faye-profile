About Me

Hello! I'm Faye, a 20-year-old second-year Bachelor of Arts in Multimedia Studies (BAMS) student at the University of the Philippines Open University (UPOU). Between balancing asynchronous coursework and working full-time in Quezon City, I love exploring how design, code, and storytelling come together on the web.

Whether I am building responsive websites, designing digital zines, or writing fantasy stories late at night, I enjoy turning small creative ideas into colorful, interactive experiences. I built this profile page to practice clean HTML5 and CSS3 while sharing a piece of my personality.
